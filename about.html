Josefusek Global is a premier indigenous multi-disciplinary service provider delivering world-class engineering, asset integrity, exploration, and energy distribution networks.

WHO WE ARE

Pioneering Indigenous Engineering Since Conception

Strategically headquartered at No. 68 Atiku Abubakar Avenue, Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Josefusek Global has developed into an ambitious force in Nigeria's infrastructural landscape. Combining dynamic local insight with absolute adherence to international standards, we serve partners in both heavy civil constructions and specialized industrial sectors.

Our capabilities span critical upstream services—including structural fabrication, mechanical pipeline maintenance, and heavy equipment logistics—as well as complex downstream distribution, products marketing, and offshore energy exploration support.

Whether executing crucial municipal drainage networks, erecting state-of-the-art corporate complexes, or monitoring critical industrial pipeline systems, we provide unparalleled technical excellence from planning to implementation.

Our Specialized Services

Josefusek Global operates across major critical divisions, combining high-grade engineering capabilities with robust downstream energy logistics.

DIVISION 01

Upstream Operations

A Mechanical Engineering & Construction

  • Fabrication Services: Structural steel assembly and site infrastructure setup.
  • Maintenance: Heavy-duty system overhauls and equipment tuneups.

B Civil Engineering & Construction

  • Road / Bridge / Drainages: Heavy infrastructural layouts built for Akwa Ibom coastal conditions.
  • Building & Property Maintenance: Corporate structures asset longevity.

C Asset Integrity Management

  • Pipelines Services & Monitoring: System leak detection, pressure integrity, and structural monitoring.

D Equipment & Tools Logistics

  • Heavy Duty Equipment: Supply and transportation of OCTGs and robust structural pipes.
